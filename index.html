Brand Story

A personal journey from heartbreak to a global awakening of maternal light.

"I once lost a heartbeat — and found my life’s purpose. In the silence of loss, I discovered that creation begins long before birth — in the energy of the womb."

Hi, I’m Anusha C G, founder of Garbha Jyothi™ — Light from the Womb to the World. My journey began not as a coach, but as a woman who once lost a heartbeat and found her life’s purpose in that heartbreak. Six months into my marriage, I saw two pink lines. Like every dreamy girl, I imagined tiny hands, lullabies, and laughter. But soon, the doctor said there was no heartbeat. That loss broke something in me — physically, mentally, emotionally.

Searching for answers, I discovered the ancient science of Garbha Sanskar — the wisdom of preparing the womb with awareness and love. I practiced it for three months — healing my body, mind, and emotions — and conceived again. This time I didn’t just carry a baby; I carried consciousness.

Through Garbh Samvaad, I spoke to my child daily. During labour, when I whispered, “We are safe,” my baby listened. That moment became my rebirth — and Garbha Jyothi™ was born.

Today, Garbha Jyothi™ bridges ancient Indian wisdom and modern neuroscience to help women move from fear to faith, from conception to creation — to awaken Light from the Womb to the World.

Because when a woman heals herself, she heals generations.
